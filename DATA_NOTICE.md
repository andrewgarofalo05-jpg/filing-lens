# Data and attribution

The MIT license covers original project code, original documentation and synthetic fixtures.
It does not relicense third-party filings, brands, libraries or model weights.

The offline demo contains one 24-word excerpt from Apple's 2024 Form 10-K, Item 1A,
page 8, and six manually transcribed numbers from Item 8, Consolidated Statements of
Operations, page 29. The numeric table reports millions of USD; the demo multiplies each
observation by 1,000,000. Its concept labels are assigned for demonstration and should not
be confused with a verbatim Company Facts response. The live smoke check independently
confirmed the revenue concept and observations using SEC Company Facts.

Source: [Apple Inc. 2024 Form 10-K, accession 0000320193-24-000123](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019324000123/aapl-20240928.htm).
Filing date: November 1, 2024. Report date: September 28, 2024. Verified October 15, 2026.
The filing is historical; inclusion does not make it the latest available information.

The source link is part of every demo result. The excerpt is intentionally minimal.
Public availability of issuer filings does not imply all their contents are public domain.
Do not bulk redistribute downloaded filings or remove their attribution.

`tests/fixtures/research.html` is original synthetic text about a fictional company.
It is clearly separated from real SEC evidence. Tests never represent it as a real filing.
Full live downloads live only in the ignored cache; they are excluded from the release archive.
