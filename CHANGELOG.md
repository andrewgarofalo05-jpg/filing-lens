# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Unreleased

### Added
- An installable AI skill with a JSON research helper and explicit filing/period selection.
- A local SEC filing research workspace with exact passage citations and an offline demo.
- Annual revenue, net income and operating income comparisons with deterministic arithmetic.
- Optional local AI explanations, separated from verified evidence and numerical results.
- Identified, cached and rate-limited SEC access with conservative failure handling.
- Tests, a small regression evaluation, beginner guides and draft release materials.

### Known limitations
- Recent original US-GAAP 10-Ks only; heuristic section parsing and lexical retrieval.
- No custom concept mapping, amendment reconciliation, valuation or investment recommendations.
- Local model inference depends on separately installed Ollama and model hardware requirements.
